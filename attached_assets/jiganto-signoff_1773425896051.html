Scope of Work — Project Alpha

ClientAcme Corp Ltd
ProjectProject Alpha — Phase 2
Version2.1 — Final
Date11 March 2026

1. Overview

This Scope of Work outlines the deliverables, timelines, and responsibilities agreed between Jiganto Ltd and Acme Corp Ltd for the delivery of Project Alpha, Phase 2.

2. Deliverables

The following deliverables are in scope for this engagement: (a) System architecture design and documentation, (b) API integration with existing CRM platform, (c) User acceptance testing and sign-off, (d) Deployment to production environment.

3. Timeline

Work will commence on 1 April 2026 and is expected to complete by 30 June 2026, subject to timely receipt of client materials as outlined in Section 5.

4. Commercial Terms

The agreed project fee is £48,500 + VAT, payable in three instalments as detailed in the payment schedule attached hereto.

5. Client Responsibilities

The client agrees to provide timely feedback within 5 business days of each milestone delivery, access to required systems and personnel, and prompt payment in accordance with the schedule.

6. Intellectual Property

All deliverables created under this agreement shall become the property of the client upon receipt of full payment. Pre-existing IP remains the property of Jiganto Ltd.

7. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary information shared during the engagement.

8. Acceptance

By signing this document electronically, you confirm that you have read and agree to the terms set out in this Scope of Work, and that you are authorised to sign on behalf of your organisation.
