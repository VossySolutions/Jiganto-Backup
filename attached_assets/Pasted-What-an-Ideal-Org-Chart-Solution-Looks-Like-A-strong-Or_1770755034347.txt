What an Ideal Org Chart Solution Looks Like

A strong Org Chart solution should be more than just a static visualization of employees. It should be dynamic, actionable, integrated, and collaboration‑ready. Below is a breakdown of the key capabilities that make such a solution compelling.

🧠 1. Core Functional Capabilities
✅ Interactive, Multi‑View Organizational Chart

Hierarchical view (classic top‑down tree of leaders → teams).

Flat view (ideal for modern, matrix or agile org structures).

Functional view (by department, by role, by project team).

Custom filtering (location, department, project, skills, status).

Zoom, expand/collapse, search, drag‑and‑drop.

Enables users to explore company structure intuitively — whether a company has 10 people or 100,000.

🔁 2. Full Integration With Jiganto Modules

This is what makes your Org Chart sticky and attractive:

🔹 User & Role Sync with Identity Management

Auto‑sync employees from HR/Identity modules.

Support for SSO, Azure AD, Google Workspace integrations.

🔹 Project Management Integration

Show project assignments and team compositions.

Track open roles, allocations, workloads.

🔹 Resource & Capacity Planning

Visualize resource availability.

Identify overloads and gaps across teams.

🔹 Performance & Goals

Integrate employee performance scores.

View OKRs and KPIs under each role.

🔹 Hiring & Onboarding

Highlight open positions.

Smooth HR flow from recruitment → org placement.

🔹 Permission / Security Model

Role-based access for viewing / editing.

Org Chart respects departmental privacy and data governance.

🎯 3. Smart Features that Sell
✅ AI‑Powered Insights

Automated org anomalies (duplicate roles, reporting cycles).

Predict potential restructuring based on growth trends.

Suggest optimal team structures from workload patterns.

✅ Scenario Modeling & “What‑If” Planning

Let leaders test changes:

Reassign roles.

Merge teams.

Project growth impact.

See visualization changes in real‑time.

✅ Versioning & Change History

Track restructures over time.

Compare past and current structures.

Audit trail for compliance.

✅ Multi‑Format Export

PDF/PNG for org charts.

CSV/Excel for data export.

API access for third‑party tools.
